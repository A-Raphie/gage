02 · mechanism

Four steps, zero trusted parties

01

The gage locks

Maker posts terms on Creditcoin: amount, counterparty, expiry, reference. The gage locks in the same transaction.

02

The payment lands on Sepolia

The counterparty pays into the escrow. One unambiguous event carries the deal id.

03

Attestcoin proves it

Attestors witness the block. Merkle and continuity proofs verify through precompile 0x0FD2.

04

The gage releases

The contract decodes the proved payment, matches the terms, pays out. Same transaction.

pay(sepolia) → attest(cc3) → prove(0x0FD2) → release(cc3)

03 · attestcoin depth

The protocol is the mechanism, not a garnish

Synchronous precompile verification

The ASC calls the native query verifier precompile 0x0FD2 directly. Inclusion and continuity checked on-chain, in one Creditcoin block.

Proof of the exact transaction

Merkle inclusion plus block continuity pin the exact Sepolia payment. The contract matches amount and reference against the deal terms.

Batch settlement

Up to 10 payments proved and released in one Creditcoin transaction.

Permissionless settlement

No privileged relayer. Proofs are deduplicated on-chain. Anyone can submit.
